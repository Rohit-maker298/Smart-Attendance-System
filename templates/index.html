Three steps, one look

Every check-in follows the same short sequence — designed so a match can never be faked with a printed photo or a screen.

01 / LOOK

Face the camera

The classroom camera detects a face and hands it to the recognition engine — no button to press, no card to tap.

02 / MATCH

Get identified

An ArcFace embedding is compared against every enrolled student in milliseconds, and the closest match above threshold is named.

03 / CONFIRM

Blink to verify

A short liveness check waits for a genuine blink before marking you present — a still photo held up to the lens never passes.

Instant recognition

Matches run on-device against pre-encoded enrollment photos, no cloud round-trip required to identify a face.

Liveness built in

An independent eye-landmark model watches for a real blink, kept deliberately separate from identity matching.

Live dashboard

Every verified check-in appears on the dashboard in real time, with confidence score and timestamp.

One-click export

Pull the day's attendance as a spreadsheet, ready to hand to a teacher or file with the office.
